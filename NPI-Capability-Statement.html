National Deployment & Remote Capability
We go where the project is — urban, regional, or remote. Any state, any terrain.
National ReachAble to mobilise and deploy construction teams to any location in Australia — metro, regional, or remote. Our model is built for mobility — we establish local supply chains, source trades, and manage logistics wherever the project demands.
Current OperationsVIC: 13 active residential projects across Melbourne metro and regional Victoria. QLD: Active operations in Cape York (Seisia, Northern Peninsula Area) — community supermarket and remote infrastructure.
Remote ConstructionPurpose-built for Australia's toughest environments. Cyclone-rated (Wind Region A–D), elevated builds for flood zones, termite-resistant materials, tropical design (ventilation, shade, passive cooling), extreme heat/cold adaptation.
Modular / Rapid DeployPrefabricated and modular housing capability (Panellock system) — manufactured off-site, transported and assembled on location. Reduces on-site construction time by up to 50% in remote areas.
Freight & LogisticsEstablished partnership with Sea Swift (barge freight — Cairns to Cape York/Torres Strait, weekly). Experience managing material supply to areas with limited road access, seasonal closures, and extreme remoteness.
Community & IndigenousDirector Harsimran Singh has direct relationships in Cape York Indigenous communities. Committed to local employment, skills training, and genuine community engagement — not just contractor fly-in/fly-out.
Adaptable Build TypesWe don't specialise in one type — we adapt to whatever the project requires: residential, commercial, community facilities, infrastructure, NDIS/SDA housing, social housing, government contracts, private developments.
ComplianceNCC/BCA compliance across all climate zones and wind regions. NATSPEC specifications. Understanding of Indigenous housing programs (NPARIH, National Partnership on Remote Housing). NDIS SDA design standards.
